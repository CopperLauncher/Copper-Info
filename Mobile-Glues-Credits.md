# MobileGlues credits

Copper Launcher ships the [MobileGlues](https://github.com/MobileGL-Dev/MobileGlues) OpenGL renderer and exposes its settings inside the launcher. All of the work below belongs to the people who did it.

- **Developers:** Swung, BZLZHH, Tungsten
- **License:** GNU LGPL-2.1
- **Logo:** Aou156

Everyone who wrote code, fixed bugs, translated or filled in the compatibility tables is listed, ordered by number of commits (the number under each name). The lists were generated from GitHub and reflect the day they were made.
